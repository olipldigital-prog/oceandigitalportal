Automatic inactivity rule: If a worker has no attendance for 3 consecutive calendar days, the system automatically deactivates the QR ID. The daily check runs around 1:00 AM IST after those 3 full missed days. The QR will be rejected at the gate until an Admin reactivates it.
Admin reactivation procedure: Open this Worker Directory, search the worker by ID/name, confirm the worker is approved to return, then click the red AUTO-BLOCKED (Admin Enable) status button once. Confirm it changes to ACTIVE. The admin action syncs the status to the Google Sheets database; ask the worker to scan again.
QR scan troubleshooting: If the portal says “Unknown QR / ID Code”, confirm the exact ID No printed on the card exists in the Google Sheets Workers tab. Register/sync the worker or use Settings → Sync Now. Reprint the card if its QR does not encode the same ID No.
